Meet Sasha

Hi there! I'm Alexandra (Sasha), founder of Happy Adventures by Sasha. I'm a certified travel advisor and an independent agent with Cornerstone Collective dba Cornerstone Travel, a Travel Leaders Network Affiliate Agency.

Travel has always been more than just a vacation for me — it's been a way of life. Born and raised in the Czech Republic, I spent several years living in England before eventually settling in the United States over 26 years ago. Experiencing different cultures firsthand gave me a deep love for exploring the world, and now I get to share that passion by helping others plan their dream trips.

I specialize in family travel planning designed to make vacations enjoyable, seamless, and stress-free for everyone. From choosing the right destination and resort to coordinating activities for different age groups, I focus on creating trips that balance relaxation, fun, and quality time together. Whether you're planning a Caribbean getaway, an all-inclusive resort stay, or a family-friendly cruise, I help you find options that fit your needs, preferences, and budget.

I also assist with multi-generational trips, ensuring every detail is thoughtfully planned so everyone — from kids to grandparents — has a memorable experience. My goal is simple: to take the stress out of planning so your family can focus on making lasting memories together.

Languages: English, Czech
